# Chiikawa catalog seed sources

Collected on 2026-09-28. 33 verified product records across 8 editorial series groups; these are 已收录款式, not a complete catalog. 3 regular plush and 30 plush mascots/charms. Each JSON record includes its original official name, exact published handle, product page, official CDN image URL, locally downloaded 640×640 image, material/size description, and character. Chinese names are editorial translations.

Source: Chiikawa Market official shop, https://chiikawamarket.jp/ . Public product feeds from /collections/chiikawababy/products.json and /collections/mascot/products.json supplied the product records and original image URLs. Raw source JSON is retained beside the catalog. Photo downloads all succeeded and macOS sips decoded all 33 as 640×640 images. Sample Baby plush and pajama charm were visually inspected.
